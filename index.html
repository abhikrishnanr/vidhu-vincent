01

A LIFE IN FRAMES

Look closely.
Stay with the story.

Vidhu Vincent’s work moves between journalism, documentary, cinema, writing and public communication — carrying the same instinct across each: to look closely at lives that institutions and mainstream narratives often overlook.

Her practice grew from television journalism into documentary and fiction filmmaking, then onto the page and into strategic communication. Across those forms, the medium changes; the attention to public life remains.

05

THE WORK, BEYOND THE FRAME

REPORTING → FILM

Journalism was the first camera.

Before feature filmmaking, Vincent worked in television journalism. That reporting instinct—attention to labour, gender, caste, institutions and the people living their consequences—became a foundation for her documentary and fiction work.

“I dedicate this award for all the manhole workers in Kerala.”

— Vidhu Vincent, after Manhole’s IFFK recognition, 2016
ON THE PAGE

Daivam Olivil Poya Naalukal

Her travel writing received the Kerala Sahitya Akademi Award for Travelogue, extending an observational practice from screen to prose.

MANHOLE · 2016

A debut that shifted history

Manhole won the FIPRESCI Prize for Best Malayalam Film and the Silver Crow Pheasant for Best Debut Director at IFFK. It later received Kerala State Film Awards for Best Film and Best Director.
